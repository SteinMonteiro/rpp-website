Closing the know‑do gap

Every year, institutions invest billions of dollars and people contribute millions of hours to generate social science research. Yet, up to 80% of social science research is never used. Evidence-based insights take an average of 17 years to reach the communities that need them most. Knowledge does not block progress—translation and implementation do. We align research agendas with policymakers and practitioners so research drives real-world impact.

Up to 50% of published academic papers are only read by their authors and peer reviewers.
31% of policy research reports are never downloaded.

Our core services

Research mobilization

Organizing applied research for public action

  • Applied frameworks: we design tools and prepare policy briefs for immediate real-world utility.
  • Stakeholder co-design: we bring researchers and impacted communities together from day one.
  • Ecosystem mapping: we identify where research can yield the highest return for public good.

Strategic convening

Uniting key players across policy, practice, and research

  • Targeted working groups: we facilitate sessions between researchers, government officials, and civic leaders.
  • Issue-driven campaigns: we elevate key policy questions through public discourse and digital platforms.
  • Co-designed strategy: we create structured environments where practitioners and researchers align.

Training & professional development

Capacity building for lasting social impact

  • For researchers: masterclasses on research translation, policymaker communication, and media engagement.
  • For practitioners: we will show you how to develop and design research projects for impact.
  • End-to-end mentorship: we guide project teams from ideation through policy adoption.
